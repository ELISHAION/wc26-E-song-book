Dear Beloved,

What a beautiful journey it has been! ❤️

We have already experienced three wonderful days filled with God's presence, heartfelt worship, beautiful fellowship, and precious soul food through His Word.

From the leper, to Zacchaeus, to the woman who suffered from bleeding for 12 years — each message has touched our hearts and reminded us of His unfailing love, grace, and power.

What a joy it is to be in His presence! The freedom to lift our hands, raise our voices, sing with all our hearts, and dance like David before the Lord! These are moments that make our hearts rejoice and our souls come alive in Him.

But our beautiful journey continues!

ONLY 2 MORE DAYS TO GO! ❤️

These next two days are precious. Let's not spend them simply counting down. Let's spend them drawing closer to God, soaking in His presence, preparing our hearts, and allowing Him to speak to us.

Let's set aside our distractions, surrender our worries, spend time in prayer, meditate on His Word, and develop a deeper hunger for His presence.

May our hearts be ready to rejoice, our hands be ready to worship, our feet be ready to dance, and our souls be ready to receive everything God has prepared for us.

Let's come to WC26 – WORSHIP CONFERENCE with joyful hearts, great expectations, and an unshakable desire to experience more of Him.

One God. One Family. One Heart in Worship.

See you at WC26 Day - 4! ❤️

LET EVERYTHING THAT HAS BREATH PRAISE THE LORD!

ALL GLORY BELONGS TO GOD ALONE!
