Dear Beloved,

Only 2 days to go! ❤️

As we get ready to gather once again in His presence, let's take a moment to remember those unforgettable three days when God touched our hearts in a special way.

Do you remember the powerful messages we received?

Day 1 – The Leper (కుష్ఠురోగి)
A man whom society rejected, but Jesus was willing to touch, heal, and restore. A beautiful reminder that no one is beyond His love and grace.

Day 2 – Zacchaeus (జక్కయ్య)
A man who climbed a tree just to see Jesus, but Jesus called him by name. A reminder that when Jesus enters our lives, everything can change.

Day 3 – The Woman Who Suffered for 12 Years (పన్నెండు సంవత్సరాలుగా రక్తస్రావంతో బాధపడుతున్న స్త్రీ)
She had suffered for years, but she believed that just touching the garment of Jesus would make her well. Her faith brought healing, hope, and a new beginning.

Three days. Three powerful messages. One faithful God!

We may have come with different stories, struggles, and expectations, but God reminded us that He sees us, calls us, loves us, and has the power to transform our lives.

And now, as we count down just 2 more days, let's prepare our hearts for another beautiful encounter with Him.

May this WC be more than an event. May it be a moment of renewal, transformation, and a deeper relationship with our Lord.

One God. One Family. One Heart in Worship.

Welcome to WC! ❤️

Let's worship Him with all our hearts!

ALL GLORY BELONGS TO GOD ALONE!
