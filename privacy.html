Privacy Policy

TLC Songs Book - The Lord's Church. Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who we are

TLC Songs Book ("the app") is the song book of The Lord's Church, Bowenpally, Hyderabad, India. For questions about this policy, write to thelordschurchdata3032@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you sign in for the first time, we ask for your name, phone number and email address. We use them only so that the church knows who uses the app and can contact its members and visitors about church activities.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or any payment information, and it contains no advertising or analytics trackers.

Where your information is stored

  • Church records: your name, phone number and email are saved in a private Google Sheet managed by the church (Google is our service provider for this storage).
  • On your phone: the app keeps your name, phone number, favourite songs, recently viewed songs and your colour choice on your own device so that it works offline.
  • Website hosting: our hosting provider (Vercel) may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address, when the app loads its files.

How we use and share it

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with anyone outside the church team, except the service providers named above who store or deliver it for us.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as you use the app, or until you ask us to delete them.

Delete your data

You can ask us to delete your name, phone number and email at any time. Send an email to thelordschurchdata3032@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with (or mention your phone number). We delete the entry within 14 days and confirm by email. You can also tap "Sign out" in the app to remove the details stored on your phone.

Children

The app is a general church song book. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 without a parent or guardian.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
